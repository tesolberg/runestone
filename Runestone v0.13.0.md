## INTRODUCTION

This is an early alpha. Feedback can be given on the https://github.com/tesolberg/runestone page, or on the Discord server.

### What is this and why did I make it?

I wanted to play sword and sorcery adventures with a game system rigged for:

- Highly skilled adventurers from the get go.
- The main goals for the players should be to accomplish their in fiction goals. In addition, there should be a slight "numbers go up" on a character sheet during play.
- Easy to read die rolls with minimal arithmetic. You roll a hand of d6s and every 4-6 is a hit. One hit is usually enough to succeed.
- Dynamic, fast and dangerous combat.
- Not a meat grinder. When things go south, you are more likely to get knocked out than die. Then you are probably sold as a slave, and your perilous path continues from there.
- Competency should be cut into the right number of slices — not too many (e.g. 50 skills), and not too few (e.g. 3 attributes).
- I wanted difference in skill to strongly impact how well characters do on difficult tasks. If your character is among the best archers in the land, you should be able to land shots that are near impossible for other characters in the game.
- No duplicate or near duplicate values: No concurrent DEX attribute, DEX save and acrobatics skill.
- No special abilities. Solutions to problems are not found in activating special abilities listed on your character sheet, but through interactions with the fictional world, treating it like a real place.
- Light scaffolding for wilderness travel, including encumbrance and slot inventory.

This is what I came up with. The game is still under development. If you read or play the game, I would love to hear your feedback.
